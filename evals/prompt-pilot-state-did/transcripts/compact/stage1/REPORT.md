# DiD result

The estimated average policy effect is -4.335694 opioid-prescribing rate points per 100,000 per month (state-clustered SE 0.437946; 95% CI [-5.221524, -3.449864]; p = 3.39829e-12).

This state-and-month fixed-effects DiD compares the post-adoption change in the 20 adopting states with the contemporaneous change in the 20 never-adopting states. The estimand is the average effect on treated states in their 24 post-policy months. Under parallel untreated trends (and no spillovers or other treatment-timed shocks), adoption reduced monthly prescribing by about 4.34 prescriptions per 100,000. The main limitation is that this causal interpretation depends on that untested counterfactual trend assumption; the single common adoption date also prevents separating policy effects from shocks specific to adopting states beginning in January 2021. Fixed effects: state and month. Clustering: state.
